About Us

At Joe Vu Lawn Service a yard is not finished when the grass is short — it is finished when the edges are crisp and the clippings are gone. Mowing, hedging and the tidy-up, handled as one job.

Specialising in lawns, hedges and garden maintenance in both domestic and commercial property. Same operator every visit, so the yard is already known — which corner stays wet, how fast the back lawn gets away over summer, where the sprinkler heads are. Quotes are given in detail before any work starts, and everything cut is cleared away before we leave.
